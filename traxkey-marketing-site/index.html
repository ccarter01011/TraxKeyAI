How it works

Four steps. Zero manual triage.

01

Resident reports it

Their own link, no app to download, no account to create. Works for a long-term tenant or a weekend guest.

02

Reads the raw description, classifies the trade, urgency, and whether it's normal wear or tenant-caused.

03

Ranked by real completion rate, rating, and cost, not who answered the phone first.

04

Dispatched, or held for you

Under your cost threshold, it goes out automatically. Over it, or an unproven vendor, it waits for one click from you.
