Why this didn't exist

Tracks everything. Holds the key.

Software categories get drawn once, early, and then everyone builds inside the lines. Long-term rentals went one way. Short-term went the other. Nobody redrew the line when operators started living on both sides of it.

The line as drawn

A lease and a booking looked like different businesses in 2010, so they became different software. AppFolio and Buildium on one side. Hostaway and Guesty on the other. Both correct at the time.

What changed

Two houses on annual leases and three cabins on Airbnb stopped being unusual. The operator didn't pick a side, so they bought both subscriptions and became the integration between them personally.

What we did

Put both in one schema. That's it. It sounds obvious, and it is — it's just structural enough that adding it later means rebuilding the middle of a working product, which is why nobody has.

We'd rather be checkable than loud. Everywhere on this site that we're behind, we say so: Breezeway's turnover tooling is deeper than ours, the big platforms' accounting is more mature, and Hostaway and Guesty are better at getting your listing in front of people.

None of that is the gap we're pointing at. Open any of those products and look for a lease table next to a booking calendar. That takes about ten minutes, and it's the whole argument.
