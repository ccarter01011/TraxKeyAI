A niche nobody else built for

5 acres, 7 cabins, one pool. That's not a normal STR listing.

A single-unit STR tool has no idea a pool exists. Hotel PMS is built for 80 rooms and a front desk, not 7 cabins run by two people. If you're comparing us to Hostaway or Guesty

They're better at channel management. This is the part they can't do.

Fair fight, not a dismissal. Hostaway and Guesty are excellent at listing syndication, and both now ship their own AI (Hostaway calls theirs "AI CoHost"). Here's exactly where that AI runs into a wall neither product can get past.

Their AI has no lease table

Hostaway and Guesty were built for the booking calendar side. Neither has a concept of a lease, a tenant, or a long-term unit. If you run any mix of long-term and short-term, their AI is answering with half your portfolio missing — structurally, not as a roadmap gap.

Maintenance coordination isn't the product

Channel managers are built to get you bookings and sync calendars across platforms. Diagnosing a reported problem, matching a vendor by real performance, and gating dispatch on cost is a different job — one Breezeway and Turno built for years, and Hostaway/Guesty haven't.

No compound or shared-amenity concept

A single-listing STR tool has no idea a pool exists. If you run a compound, glamping site, or micro-resort, there's nowhere in either product to track a shared amenity, notify every current guest at once, or book a whole-property buyout.

Pricing intelligence, without the per-listing tax

Neither includes dynamic pricing outright — you connect PriceLabs, Wheelhouse, or Beyond, each billing per listing on top of your channel-manager subscription. TraxKey's pricing tier is inside your subscription, with no separate per-listing line item.

Where they're ahead: syndication across more channels, more mature guest messaging, and years more polish on the booking side. If listing distribution is your bottleneck, they're the right tool. If maintenance, mixed portfolios, or compounds are, that's what TraxKey was built for.
