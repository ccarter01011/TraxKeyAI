# Competitor site research — 2026-08-14

Reviewed: Hostaway, Guesty, Hospitable, Lodgify (STR) and Buildium (LTR).

---

## The finding that matters most

**Every one of these has already shipped an AI assistant, and they describe
it in almost exactly the words we use.**

| Product | Their AI | Their words |
|---|---|---|
| Hostaway | **AI CoHost** | "looks across your live business data to find opportunities, suggest your next move, and get it done faster" |
| Guesty | **Data Intelligence** | "instant, plain-language answers about your revenue, occupancy, and portfolio performance, straight from your live data" |
| Hospitable | **AI Copilot** | "answers natural-language business questions across your entire portfolio in seconds" |
| Buildium | **Lumina AI** | "agentic AI for property management" |

Guesty's Data Intelligence is, feature for feature, the Portfolio Insights
spec. Hostaway's CoHost is the concierge.

### What this means

**"AI" is table stakes in this category, not a differentiator.** A prospect
landing on traxkey.ai has already seen four AI pitches this week. Leading
with "AI team of specialists" now sounds like everyone else, and worse, it
invites a feature comparison against companies with far more built.

We should keep building the AI, it is genuinely good and it is how the
product works. We should stop leading the marketing with it.

### What is still genuinely ours

Checked deliberately across all five:

- **Hostaway, Guesty, Hospitable, Lodgify: short-term only.** No leases, no
  renewals, no long-term tenants.
- **Buildium: long-term only.** Its "Made for mixed portfolios" section means
  residential + commercial + HOA, all long-term. No STR, no channel calendars.

**Nobody serves the operator running both.** That is a checkable, factual
gap, not a marketing claim, and it is the only thing on this list a prospect
cannot get elsewhere.

Second, downstream of it: **occupancy-aware maintenance urgency.** It
requires the booking calendar and the maintenance engine in one system.
Structurally impossible for an STR tool with no maintenance engine, and for
an LTR tool with no calendars.

## What each site does well

### Hostaway
- **Hard, specific numbers** as proof: "60 minutes saved per reservation",
  "25% more revenue per listing", "90% of messaging automated".
- **Named customer results** with country, portfolio size, video: "10X
  portfolio growth, 80+ properties, France".
- Support framed as a feature (24/7 phone, 97% satisfaction, six continents).
- Feature grid grouped by job (Communication, Analytics, Automation), not by
  a flat feature list.

### Guesty
- **Tiering by portfolio size, named as products**: Lite (1-3), Pro (4-199),
  Enterprise (200+). A visitor self-selects in one glance and never wonders
  "is this for someone my size".
- An interest picker above the fold ("What are you most interested in?") that
  routes people to the relevant page instead of making them hunt.

### Hospitable
- **Volume proof**: "$5.2B reservation income processed", "55k+ AI replies
  daily", "22M+ guests across 191 countries", "4.8 across G2/Capterra".
- "**We build for the hard problems, not just the easy ones**", then labels
  four features "Hospitable exclusive". Confident without being vague.
- Audience cards: Self-managers / Co-hosts & PMs / Enterprise / Property
  owners, each with its own promise.

### Lodgify
- **An interactive demo embedded directly on the homepage**, with a live
  calendar you can click, not a screenshot.
- Qualifies with a question first: "Are you currently hosting? Yes / Not yet
  / I manage for other owners", then tailors what it shows.
- Labels it honestly: "This is a simplified version of our product filled
  with data for demo purposes." Exactly the label we used on /demo.
- Onboarding as a selling point: "Free 1:1 onboarding. A real human sets you
  up." Powerful against faceless competitors.

### Buildium
- **"Made for mixed portfolios"** with a card per segment. This is the exact
  pattern our LTR/STR story needs, and it is the closest thing on any of
  these sites to our positioning.
- **Free trial seeded with sample data**: "It takes just 30 seconds. No
  credit card. Use sample data to see how Buildium handles your real-world
  tasks." Removes the empty-state problem entirely.
- Tabbed feature explorer (Accounting | Payments | Leasing | Maintenance...)
  keeps a huge feature set scannable.
- "AI **that you control**", which is close to our Business Memory argument
  and worth beating them to.

## Proposed changes to traxkey.ai

Ordered by expected impact.

### 1. Re-lead on the mixed portfolio, demote the AI
Current hero leans on the AI team of specialists. Every competitor now says
that. Lead instead on the one thing that is checkably true and unavailable
elsewhere: long-term and short-term in one system. Keep the AI as *how* it
works, not *what* it is.

### 2. A "Who this is for" section, Buildium-style
Three cards: **Long-term only / Short-term only / Both**. The third is the
hero card and says plainly that this is the one nobody else serves. Doubles
as honest qualification, an STR-only operator should know Hostaway is more
mature at STR-only work.

### 3. Put the demo on the homepage
`/demo` is good but nobody clicks a nav item to find it. Lodgify embeds it
above the fold. Move an interactive slice of it into the homepage, keep the
full page behind "see the whole dashboard".

### 4. Replace invented proof with real proof
We have no customers, so we cannot run Hostaway's numbers, and inventing
them is out. What we *can* show honestly:
- The audit trail. "Every decision, logged with its reasoning" is a
  screenshot none of these four show, because their AI is a black box.
- Time-to-first-value: "Add a unit, share a link, watch it handle the first
  request." Buildium's sample-data trial is the model.

### 5. Steal the sample-data trial
Buildium's best idea. A new account is an empty dashboard, which is the
worst possible first impression of an AI product that needs data to be
interesting. Offer "explore with sample data" on signup, seeded from the
same demo portfolio the marketing page uses.

### 6. Tier by portfolio size, visibly
Guesty names its tiers by listing count so a visitor self-selects instantly.
Ours are Free/Starter/Growth/Pro with unit counts in the fine print. Put the
unit range in the tier name.

### 7. Onboarding as a feature
Lodgify sells "a real human sets you up". At our size that is a genuine
advantage, not a cost. Say it.

## Not worth copying

- **Dynamic pricing** (Hostaway, Guesty). Needs market data we do not have,
  and PriceLabs already owns it.
- **Trust accounting** (Buildium, Guesty). Deliberately out of scope, same
  reasoning as always.
- **Channel management write-back** (all four STR tools). Gated APIs and
  partner agreements. Read-only iCal is the honest version.
- **Their proof numbers.** We have no customers. Any equivalent number on
  our site today would be fabricated.
